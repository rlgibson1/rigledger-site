RigLedger IFTA app privacy policy

The RigLedger IFTA app is made by GSA LLC (RigLedger). It collects nothing. We never receive your entries, your location, your photos or anything that identifies you. Here is exactly what the app does with your information.

No account

There is no sign-up and no sign-in. We don't know who uses the app.

What stays on your phone

Everything you put in the app is stored only in the app's own storage on your phone: state lines, odometer readings, fuel entries, notes, receipt photos, the totals you type, your settings, and the driver details you enter for the PDF (name, IFTA account number, unit number and base state). None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

The one thing the app downloads

At most once a day, when you open the app, it downloads the public IFTA rates file from rigledger.gsa-company.com/ifta-rates.json. It is a plain download of the same file anyone can open in a browser. The request carries no account, no device ID, no location and none of your entries. Like any web server, the site's host may log the request itself, such as the network address it came from and the time. That is the app's only network request.

Location

The app reads your phone's location only when you tap a log button (and once during first setup, to ask which state you're in), and only while the app is open. It never tracks you in the background. The reading is checked on the phone against state boundaries built into the app to name the state you're in, and it is kept with that entry on your phone so the app can suggest odometer numbers at state lines. It is never sent anywhere, and it leaves the phone only inside a backup file you choose to share. If you turn location off, the app asks you to pick the state instead.

Camera and receipt photos

The camera opens only when you log fuel, to photograph the receipt, and you can skip it. Photos are saved in the app's own storage, not shared anywhere. They leave the phone only if you share them yourself, in a PDF or a backup file you send through your phone's share sheet.

Files you share

PDFs, CSV files and backups are made on your phone and handed to your phone's share sheet. Where they go next is your choice (your files, your email, your cloud drive). We never get a copy.

Deadline reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone by the app itself. There is no reminder server, and turning reminders off in the app or in your phone's settings stops them.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play, which handle the payment under their own privacy policies. We never see your card details. The app asks the store whether you own the unlock, and remembers the answer on your phone.

No analytics, ads or tracking

The app has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind, and it contains no third-party code that collects data.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry in the app. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. Copies you made yourself, such as a backup file or a PDF you shared, stay wherever you put them, and so does anything in your phone's own system backups if you use them.

Children

The app is a record-keeping tool for commercial truck drivers. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone of any age.
