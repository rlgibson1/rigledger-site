RigLedger IFTA: your fuel tax log, done in the cab.

An IFTA log for iPhone and Android, built for the driver on a phone, not a laptop and a big spreadsheet. Three big buttons while you drive. Your quarterly return when you park.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

Three big buttons

  • STATE LINE

    One tap when you cross. The app saves the crossing right away and names the new state from one location reading, checked on the phone against state lines built into the app. No signal needed.

  • FUEL

    When you're parked: snap the receipt, type the gallons, and the state is filled in. Reefer fuel can be kept out of the return with one switch.

  • ODOMETER

    When you're parked: type the reading. Your state miles come from your odometer, not from GPS tracking that drops miles.

One tap while moving. Typing waits until you park.

Federal rules for commercial drivers treat more than one button press on a phone as hand-held use, and typing as texting. So a moving driver only ever taps STATE LINE. The number pad shows up only when you're stopped, and anything the app still needs goes on a short to-do list for later.

Already have your totals?

Got a state-mileage report from your ELD, or totals on paper? Choose Type my totals, enter each state's miles and tax-paid gallons, and the app works out your return. Nothing to log first.

What's free, and what the unlock adds

  • Free

    Logging, total miles, total gallons, fleet MPG, your net tax due or credit, the due date and days left, deadline reminders, and backup and restore. Your records are never held behind the paywall.

  • One-time unlock, no subscription

    The per-state return lines (miles, taxable gallons, tax-paid gallons, rate, tax and surcharge for each state), an audit PDF with your return, trip record, fuel list and receipt photos, and CSV files that paste into the RigLedger owner-operator workbook.

Official rates, refreshed free

The app carries the official IFTA diesel rates from IFTA, Inc.'s tax matrix and picks up each new quarter's rates free from this site. If a quarter's rates aren't posted yet, the app says which quarter's rates it used, on the screen and on the PDF.

Works offline. Your records stay on your phone.

No account and no sign-in. It works with no signal. Your entries, photos and settings stay on your phone. The only thing the app downloads is the public rates file. Details are in the app's privacy policy.

Who it's for

US-based owner-operators who file their own IFTA return, one truck, diesel.

It does not file the return for you, track GPS in the background, run a fleet, handle Canada-based filers, or cover gasoline or other fuels. Questions are answered on the support page.

Planning and record-keeping tool. Not tax, legal, or engineering advice.
